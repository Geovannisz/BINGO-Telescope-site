research_lines: The most mysterious nature in the universe now is the nature of
  dark matter and dark energy. In order to find it, I have tried many things.
  First, I use cosmological simulations to study the statistics of dark matter
  and dark energy. Then, I compared the simulations to existing observational
  results, but I find that if I don’t know the details in observations, signals
  may only be systematic errors in observations. Then, I joined BINGO
  collaboration, to watch and participate in the whole process of construction,
  to understand what is really behind observations. I also contributed to build
  necessary hardware for radio astronomy, just to understand what raw
  experiments are like. I have tried many detailed projects to understand dark
  matter and dark energy, and published more than 50 papers. Recently, I tried
  to combine my experience and all my skills together, building AI agents for
  cosmology. I believe it is the right way to find the answer.

project_title: 21cm Cosmology
project_description: I study cosmology using 21cm. 21cm is the signal coming
  from neutral hydrogen from the universe, which shows the distribution of
  neutral hydrogen. It contains the mystery of dark matter and dark energy,
  about the ultimate nature of our universe.
project_problem: My research aim to find out the nature of dark matter and dark energy.
project_importance: Dark matter and dark energy are known as the new two clouds
  for physics. When people solve the last two clouds for physics 100 years ago,
  we discovered quantum physics and relativity. The fundamental discovery
  changed our life deeply, and we use all technology based on these science
  everyday. Without them, we would not have the current modern life. A discovery
  leading to solve the nature of dark matter and dark energy, will lead us to
  another big jump in science and technology.
project_results: By now, it is mostly accepted that dark matter should exist and
  should be some kind of new particle that we have never discovered before, and
  there are still very little understanding about dark energy. Some people even
  do not believe dark energy exists. In my point of view, the nature of dark
  matter will lead to a new discovery of new particles, or a bunch new
  particles. And the nature of dark energy will lead to a new discovery beyond
  general relativity, about the new understanding of gravity.
project_challenges: Currently, I find it to hard to clearly find the 21cm signal
  from raw observational data. The signal is orders of magnitude smaller than
  what we receive. And we need to pick it out from garbage. The simulation
  results show really good forecasts, but the reality if much dirtier than
  simulations. We have to touch the real world to find solutions, with courage,
  wisdom and unity.
biggest_curiosity: My biggest curiosity is not the knowledge of the universe
  itself, but the path toward knowledge. There exists the truth of the universe,
  maybe it is the nature of dark matter and dark energy, maybe not, maybe more.
  However, curiosity drive me into research, I want to know the logic of the
  world. I want to know how we can find the truth of the universe, the process
  itself is more important than the answer.
common_myth: I work in Shanghai Astronomical Observatory, when people talk about
  observatory, usually they will think about counting stars everyday in the
  night, maybe on top of a mountain. In fact, most our daily life is writing
  software and performing data analysis. Our telescopes can mostly control
  remotely, we can also do simulations in supercomputers to simulate the
  evolution of the universe. We don't count stars at night. I can only identify
  1 or 2 constellations in the sky.
impressive_discovery: In recent years, many telescopes have been constructed and
  used to measure remote galaxies, to look for the properties of dark matter and
  dark energy. First of all, we can accurately measure the amount of dark matter
  dark energy to percent level. Secondly, as shown by the latest observations,
  we find the hint that dark energy may not be a simple constant, but dynamical.
  Therefore, we not only entering an era that precisely measure the parameters
  of cosmology, but also open up the opportunity to look for more bizarre models
  beyond the current standard model.
