project_importance: The project is important because it explores beyond the
  standard ΛCDM framework, investigating how alternative cosmological
  models—particularly those involving interactions between dark matter and dark
  energy—modify the growth of cosmic structures and leave distinct imprints on

  statistics of large-scale structure and black hole mergers to constrain
  cosmological parameters and illuminate the nature of dark matter and dark
  energy.
